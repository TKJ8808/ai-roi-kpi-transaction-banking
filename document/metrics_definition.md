# KPI & ROI Definitions – Transaction Banking Automation

## Average Processing Time
Mean time (in minutes) required to process a transaction case end-to-end.

## Manual Review Rate
Percentage of cases that require human intervention.

## Error Rate
Percentage of cases where processing errors occurred.

## SLA Breach Rate
Percentage of cases that exceeded agreed service-level timelines.

---

## ROI Methodology

ROI is calculated based on operational time savings achieved through automation.

### Key Assumptions
- 22 working days per month
- Fixed operational cost per hour
- Automation cost includes infrastructure and maintenance
- Data used is synthetic but operationally realistic

### Purpose
This methodology enables decision-makers to:
- Quantify automation benefits
- Compare pre- and post-automation performance
- Support investment justification in regulated environments
