# Feedback Analysis – Adoption & Change Management

## Objective
To analyze operational user feedback and assess adoption of AI automation
within transaction banking operations.

## Approach
- Collect qualitative feedback from operators
- Apply NLP-based sentiment analysis
- Identify common pain points and improvement areas

## Sentiment Categories
- Positive: Indicates efficiency gains and satisfaction
- Neutral: Suggests usability improvements
- Negative: Highlights operational risks or adoption barriers

## Business Value
- Supports change adoption strategies
- Enables targeted system improvements
- Complements KPI and ROI metrics with qualitative insights

## Responsible AI
- Feedback analysis is advisory only
- No automated personnel decisions
- Insights are aggregated and anonymized
